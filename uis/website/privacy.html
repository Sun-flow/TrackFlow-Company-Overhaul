What the information request form collects

When you request information, we ask for your company's name and website, the name, email and phone number of a contact person, your main operating country, the type of product you handle, your estimated monthly shipping volume, the services you are interested in, whether you already work with another logistics provider, and any comments you choose to add.

We use this information only to contact your company about TrackFlow's logistics services and to understand your needs before that conversation.
