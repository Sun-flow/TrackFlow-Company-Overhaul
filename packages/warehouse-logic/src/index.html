Validations

Load a sample, change any value in its JSON, and validate it. The input validator checks the record's shape first, then the strict rules, and never throws. Once the shape passes, the briefing's validators check its 14 rules, and the strict ones beside them also check finite numbers, text patterns, real dates and values on their lists.

A shipment's createdAt is written as a date in text, such as "2024-03-15".
