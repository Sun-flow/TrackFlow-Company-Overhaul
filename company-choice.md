# Reasoning:

I find the real-world applications of distribution logistics to be a very important part of understanding networking, AI, and global flow of commerce, so I feel that this company will expose me to many thoughts regarding those topics that will benefit me going forward. Additionally, shipping interfaces are highly readable and interpretable by both humans and technology, due to their innate nature of relying on geospatial data. This will be beneficial in allowing me to create impressive or useful displays and readouts, as well as manage computational problems around navigation and infrastructure dependency.

# Exciting Challenge

I'm really looking forward to building hierarchical agent systems that are capable of problem identification and solution automatically. In a system such as this with so many moving parts, being able to build clean interaction modes between the moving parts (agents) will make or break the project. It seems like this will come about in milestone 9, Agentic workflows.

# My AI Agent idea

The entire system will need to be able to feed information into at least one level of "orchestrator" agent, that is capable of triggering that activities of other agents by providing them relevant input. Rather than needing to know how to do anything directly itself, this "orchestrator" will be aware of all of the capabilities of the agents within its purview such that it can route the relevant information between them as it arises. Orchestrator agent will need a few things:

- Always listening / able to be prompted
- Understands the macro-structure of the company's agents
- Has clear ways of ingesting information from every agent in the company
- Has clear ways of prompting any active agents in the company
- Strong guardrails for ensuring that management of individual agents does not get messy or cause operational issues
- Capable of interacting with users in the company to manage the individual subagents, or carry out tasks using them
